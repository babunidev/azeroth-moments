# Azeroth Moments — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

Azeroth Moments turns gameplay screenshots and video into automatically published records of a player's adventures in World of Warcraft. The desired interaction is almost invisible: capture the moment, place it in the watched folder, and let a local service prepare and distribute it.

Discord should preserve the exact original; Instagram requires a format-aware presentation that does not simply throw away the composition. Wide images become geometry-driven carousel panels, while landscape videos can become a preview-first multi-panel experience.

**Original direction:** [Initial brief](docs/BRIEF.md), [adaptive-image addendum](docs/BRIEF_ADDENDUM.md) and [video addendum](docs/VIDEO_ADDENDUM.md).

## How the creative idea developed

The original brief asks for screenshot sharing that feels like a single action: place a game capture in a folder. The next brief addresses the author's freeform region captures, making arbitrary image shapes a first-class input rather than forcing fullscreen assumptions. The third brief brings video into the same artistic publishing pipeline: preserve originals on Discord while translating landscape movement into a preview-first sequence on Instagram.

Those are successive user directions, not new ideas invented by this audit. The resulting publishing architecture is AI-assisted technical implementation; author intent and verified results must remain separate.
